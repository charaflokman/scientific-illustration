اعمل لي ملخص رسومي (graphical abstract) لورقتي عن أفلام الكيتوزان مع النانوسليلوز، من فقرة المنهجية هذه. أريده قابل للتعديل لأن المشرف سيطلب تغييرات:

"Chitosan (medium molecular weight, DD 85%) was dissolved at 2% (w/v) in 1% (v/v) acetic acid under magnetic stirring for 24 h at room temperature. A cellulose nanocrystal (CNC) suspension was added to obtain 1, 3 and 5 wt% CNC relative to chitosan, and the mixture was sonicated for 10 min. Glycerol (30 wt% of chitosan) was added as plasticizer and stirred for 30 min. Then 25 mL of the film-forming solution was cast into 9 cm Petri dishes and dried at 40 °C. Films were conditioned at 50% RH before characterization by FTIR, XRD, tensile testing and water vapour permeability."
