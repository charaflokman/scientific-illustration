حوّل هذا النص إلى مخطط تدفق (flowchart) نظيف أضعه في فصل المنهجية من رسالتي، وأريد أن أعدّل نصه لاحقاً بسهولة:

"Bacterial cellulose was produced by Komagataeibacter xylinus in Hestrin-Schramm medium for 7 days at 30 °C under static conditions. The pellicles were harvested, washed with distilled water, and treated with 0.1 M NaOH at 80 °C for 1 h to remove cells, then washed until neutral pH. Part of the pellicles was freeze-dried for characterization (SEM, FTIR, XRD); the rest was impregnated with ZnO nanoparticle suspensions at two concentrations and tested for antibacterial activity against E. coli and S. aureus by the disc diffusion method."
